>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  SECTION 1: PROJECT GOAL
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  Build a self-hosted automation system where:

    1. A customer sends a message to the business WhatsApp number.
    2. An AI assistant automatically chats with the customer.
    3. The AI answers common business questions.
    4. The AI collects the customer's lead information.
    5. The system automatically creates or updates the lead in Odoo CRM.
    6. Everything (except the official WhatsApp service) runs on a local PC.

  Constraint: Use free and self-hosted software wherever possible.

>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  SECTION 2: IMPORTANT MEANING OF 'FREE'
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  [A] PROHIBITED (Must NOT Use):
    - OpenAI API
    - Paid n8n Cloud
    - Paid hosting or VPS
    - Odoo Enterprise
    - Paid chatbot platforms
    - Paid database services

  [B] REQUIRED (Use These Instead):
    - n8n Community Edition          (self-hosted automation)
    - Ollama                         (local AI model runtime)
    - Odoo Community Edition         (on-premise CRM)
    - PostgreSQL                     (local database)
    - Docker & Docker Compose        (containerization)
    - Cloudflare Quick Tunnel        (public webhook for testing)
    - Meta WhatsApp Cloud API        (messaging service)

  Note: WhatsApp service messages sent during the 24-hour customer-service
        window are currently not charged by Meta. Marketing, authentication,
        or business-initiated messages may still incur a cost.

  SECTION 4: MAIN SYSTEM WORKFLOW
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

    Step 1:   Customer sends a WhatsApp message
    Step 2:   Meta WhatsApp Cloud API receives the message
    Step 3:   Meta sends the message to the n8n webhook
    Step 4:   n8n checks whether the message was already processed
    Step 5:   n8n loads the customer's previous conversation
    Step 6:   n8n sends the message + history to Ollama
    Step 7:   Ollama returns:
                 - WhatsApp reply
                 - Collected lead information
                 - Lead status
                 - Human handover status
    Step 8:   n8n sends the reply to the customer on WhatsApp
    Step 9:   n8n saves the conversation
    Step 10:  If enough lead information is available:
                 - Search for the customer in Odoo
                 - Create a new lead or update the existing lead
                 - Create a follow-up activity for the salesperson

>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  SECTION 5: CUSTOMER CONVERSATION FLOW
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  The AI should communicate naturally and ask only one or two questions at a time.

  [Data to Collect]:
    - Customer name
    - WhatsApp phone number
    - Company name (when relevant)
    - Product or service of interest
    - Customer requirement
    - Location
    - Approximate budget
    - Required timeline
    - Email address (when available)
    - Preferred contact time
    - Additional notes

  [Example Conversation]:
    Customer : "I need an ERP system."
    AI       : "Thank you for contacting us. What type of business do you operate?"
    Customer : "We have a wholesale business."
    AI       : "Understood. Which areas do you want to manage, such as sales,
                inventory, accounting, or purchasing?"
    Customer : "Sales, stock and accounts."
    AI       : "Thank you. May I know your name and company name?"

  Rule: The AI must NOT ask for information already provided by the customer.

>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  SECTION 6: AI BEHAVIOUR RULES
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

    1.  Reply in the same language used by the customer when possible.
    2.  Support simple Bangla and English.
    3.  Be polite, short, and professional.
    4.  Ask questions one at a time.
    5.  Never invent a price, feature, policy, or delivery date.
    6.  Only use approved business information.
    7.  Inform the customer when information is unavailable.
    8.  Transfer the conversation to a human when necessary.
    9.  Do not request passwords, payment PINs, OTPs, or sensitive financial data.
    10. Create or update the Odoo lead when useful information is available.
    11. Do not wait until the conversation is completely finished before saving the lead.
    12. Update the same lead when the customer provides more information.

>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  SECTION 7: AI RESPONSE FORMAT (Ollama JSON)
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  Ollama must return valid JSON instead of unstructured text.

  Required Format:
  {
      "reply": "Thank you. What is your approximate budget?",
      "lead_ready": false,
      "human_handoff": false,
      "handoff_reason": "",
      "lead_score": 40,
      "lead": {
          "name": "Rahim",
          "phone": "8801XXXXXXXXX",
          "email": "",
          "company": "ABC Traders",
          "product_interest": "Odoo ERP",
          "requirement": "Sales, inventory and accounting",
          "budget": "",
          "timeline": "",
          "location": "Dhaka",
          "preferred_contact_time": "",
          "notes": ""
      }
  }

  [Invalid JSON Handling]:
    1. Retry the AI request once.
    2. Use a safe fallback reply if the second attempt fails.
    3. Save the error in the execution log.
    4. Avoid creating incorrect Odoo data.

  Fallback Reply: "Thank you for your message. We are having a temporary
                    technical issue. A team member will review your request."

>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  SECTION 8: LEAD-READY RULES
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  Set lead_ready = true when at least the following are available:
    - Phone number
    - Customer name or identifiable contact
    - Product or service interest
    - Basic customer requirement

  Also trigger immediate lead creation/update when the customer says:
    - "Call me"
    - "I need a quotation"
    - "I am interested"
    - "Please arrange a meeting"
    - "I want to talk to a salesperson"
    - "How can I buy?"
    - Similar high-intent messages

>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>
  SECTION 9: HUMAN HANDOVER RULES
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  Set human_handoff = true when:
    - The customer asks for a human agent.
    - The customer makes a complaint.
    - The customer is angry or unhappy.
    - The AI does not know the correct answer.
    - The customer requests a final price or negotiation.
    - The customer requests a customised quotation.
    - The customer asks legal, payment, or contract questions.
    - The AI fails repeatedly.

  [Handover Procedure]:
    1. Stop normal AI sales questions.
    2. Create or update the Odoo lead.
    3. Create an Odoo follow-up activity.
    4. Add the handover reason.
    5. Send the handover reply to the customer.

  Handover Reply: "Thank you. I have shared your request with our team.
                      A representative will contact you as soon as possible."

  SECTION 11: n8n WORKFLOW STRUCTURE
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  Create one main workflow with the following nodes:

    Node   Function
    ----   --------------------------------------------------------
     1     Webhook - Meta verification
     2     Webhook - Incoming WhatsApp message
     3     Code/Set - Extract message information
     4     IF - Ignore status updates and unsupported events
     5     Database lookup - Check duplicate message ID
     6     Database lookup - Load conversation history
     7     Database lookup - Load business information
     8     HTTP Request - Send request to Ollama
     9     Code - Parse and validate AI JSON
    10     IF - Check AI response validity
    11     HTTP Request - Send reply through WhatsApp Cloud API
    12     Database - Save incoming and outgoing messages
    13     IF - Check lead_ready or human_handoff
    14     HTTP Request/Code - Search Odoo lead
    15     IF - Existing lead found
    16     HTTP Request/Code - Update Odoo lead
    17     HTTP Request/Code - Create Odoo lead
    18     Odoo action - Create salesperson follow-up activity
    19     Database - Mark message as processed
    20     Error workflow - Save and report errors

  SECTION 12: CONVERSATION STORAGE (Database Schema)
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  Use PostgreSQL or another reliable local database.

  [Table: contacts]
    - id
    - phone_number
    - customer_name
    - email
    - company
    - language
    - ai_enabled
    - human_handoff
    - created_at
    - updated_at

  [Table: messages]
    - id
    - whatsapp_message_id
    - phone_number
    - direction
    - message_text
    - message_type
    - created_at

  [Table: conversations]
    - id
    - phone_number
    - conversation_summary
    - collected_lead_data
    - lead_score
    - odoo_lead_id
    - last_message_at
    - status

  [Table: processed_messages]
    - whatsapp_message_id
    - processed_at

  Note: The processed_messages table prevents duplicate replies when Meta
        resends a webhook event.

  SECTION 14: PUBLIC WEBHOOK (Cloudflare Quick Tunnel)
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  For the free testing version, expose the local n8n webhook through
  Cloudflare Quick Tunnel.

  Example Command:
    cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:5678

  Caveats:
    - Quick Tunnels are intended only for testing and development.
    - They generate a random public subdomain.
    - No uptime guarantee is provided.
    - The webhook URL may need to be updated after restarting the tunnel.

  [Documentation Required]:
    - How to start the tunnel
    - How to find the new public URL
    - Where to update the URL in Meta
    - How to verify the WhatsApp webhook
    - What happens when the PC or internet is offline

  SECTION 15: SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

    1.  Keep Meta access tokens outside workflow screenshots.
    2.  Store credentials in n8n Credentials or environment variables.
    3.  Protect the n8n login with a strong password.
    4.  Do not expose Odoo or PostgreSQL directly to the public internet.
    5.  Validate Meta webhook verification requests.
    6.  Validate incoming payloads.
    7.  Limit accepted message types.
    8.  Add duplicate-message protection.
    9.  Keep regular database backups.
    10. Remove tokens and passwords from exported workflow files.
    11. Add a method to disable the AI assistant manually.

  SECTION 19: FREE-VERSION LIMITATIONS
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  The owner understands that:

    - The PC must remain turned on.
    - The internet connection must remain active.
    - The chatbot stops when the PC, n8n, tunnel, or internet is offline.
    - Local AI response speed depends on PC hardware.
    - A small local AI model may be less accurate than a paid cloud model.
    - Cloudflare Quick Tunnel is for testing only; no guaranteed production uptime.
    - Restarting the free tunnel may produce a new webhook URL.
    - WhatsApp marketing or business-initiated template messages may incur Meta charges.
    - Electricity and internet costs are not included in 'free software'.
    - This setup is suitable for an MVP and low message volume.

  SECTION 20: FINAL EXPECTED RESULT
>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>>

  The final system should work as follows:

    Customer sends message on WhatsApp
        -> Local n8n receives the message
        -> Local Ollama AI prepares the reply
        -> Reply is sent to WhatsApp
        -> Lead information is collected
        -> Odoo lead is created or updated
        -> Salesperson receives a follow-up task

  No paid AI API, paid automation platform, paid server, or Odoo Enterprise
  subscription should be required for the MVP.
